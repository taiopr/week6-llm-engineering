Customer Feedback Report - Q1 2025
Product: AI Writing Assistant Product
Summary of 47 responses collected via in-app survey and support tickets.

Positive Feedback:
- 31 customers praised the autocomplete speed, callit it "instant" and "snappy"
- 24 customers highlighted the tone adjustment feature as ther most-used tool
- 18 customers reported significant time savings, averaging 2.5 hours per week
- Several enterprise customers noted smooth integration with their existing workflow

Negative Feedback:
- 19 customers reported the tool frequently suggests generic, templated phrasing
- 14 customers complained about loss of personal writing voice after extended use
- 11 customers experieced context loss in documents longer than 3,000 words
- 8 customers reported the mobile version crashes on iOS 17. specifically
- 6 enterprise customers flagged GDPR compliance documentation as incomplete

Feature Requests:
- 22 customers requested a "learning mode" that adapts to individual writing style
- 15 customers want offline mode for sensitive document handling
- 12 customers requested Notion and Confluence integration
- 9 customers asked for version history and undo at the suggestion level

Church Risk:
- 6 enterprise accounts (representing ~$180k ARR) have flagged dissatisfaction
- 3 of these specifically cited GDPR documentation as a blocker for continued use
- Support ticket volume increased 34% this quarter vs last
